SALVA FOODS READY WEBSITE

1. Open index.html to preview the website.
2. Upload index.html, style.css, script.js, robots.txt and sitemap.xml to your hosting public_html folder.
3. Replace YOUR-DOMAIN.com in robots.txt and sitemap.xml with the real domain.
4. Replace the placeholder phone, WhatsApp, email, address and company text with confirmed Salva Foods details.
5. After the site is live, add the domain to Google Search Console and submit /sitemap.xml.

Google indexing can make the site searchable, but a particular ranking/position is not guaranteed.
